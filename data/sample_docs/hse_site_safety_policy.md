# HSE Site Safety Policy

*Northwind Infrastructure (fictional sample company) - Document HSE-001, revision 4*

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, subcontractors and visitors on Northwind construction sites,
energy plants and offices.

## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

Hard hats, safety boots and high-visibility vests are mandatory in all active construction zones.
Safety glasses and hearing protection are required when operating cutting, drilling or grinding tools.
Visitors must be escorted and receive PPE from the site security office before entering.

## Incident Reporting

Every incident, injury or near miss must be reported to the site HSE officer within 24 hours.
Serious injuries must be reported immediately by phone to the HSE emergency line 1500.
Incidents are recorded in the HSE incident register and investigated within 5 working days.

## Working at Height

Work above 2 metres requires a valid permit to work and a certified fall-arrest harness.
Scaffolding must be inspected and tagged by a competent person every 7 days.

## Training

All new site staff complete the 4-hour HSE induction before their first shift.
Refresher HSE training is required every 12 months.
