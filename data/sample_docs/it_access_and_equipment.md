# IT Access and Equipment Procedure

*Northwind Infrastructure (fictional sample company) - Document IT-014, revision 2*

## New Accounts

New employee accounts are requested by the line manager through the IT service portal at least
5 working days before the start date. Accounts include email, Microsoft Teams and SharePoint access.

## Laptops and Equipment

Standard employees receive a business laptop with Windows 11 and Microsoft 365.
Engineers and data teams may request a high-performance laptop with manager approval.
Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT service desk within 2 hours.

## VPN and Remote Access

Remote access to internal systems requires the corporate VPN with multi-factor authentication (MFA).
VPN access is requested through an IT ticket and is approved by the line manager.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are changed every 180 days.
Passwords must never be shared, including with IT staff.

## Software Requests

Software not in the approved catalogue is requested through an IT ticket and reviewed by the
IT security team within 3 working days.
