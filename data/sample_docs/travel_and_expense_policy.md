# Travel and Expense Policy

*Northwind Infrastructure (fictional sample company) - Document FIN-007, revision 6*

## Approval

Business travel must be approved by the line manager before booking.
International travel also requires approval by the department director.

## Accommodation and Meals

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 150 EUR per night in Athens and up to 120 EUR per night elsewhere in Greece.
The daily meal allowance for business travel is 40 EUR.

## Submitting Expenses

Expense claims are submitted in the SAP Concur expense tool within 30 days of the trip, with itemized receipts.
Claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll after Finance approval.

## Mileage

Use of a private car for business trips is reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre.
